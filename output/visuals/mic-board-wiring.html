220 Ω: series LED data resistor; either direction.
1 kΩ / 10 kΩ: not required. Mic SD uses the ESP32’s internal pull-down.
Power: keep 3V3 and 5V rails separate; never power the mic from 5V.
Signal reliability: if direct 3.3V LED data is unreliable, add a 5V-powered 74AHCT125. Resistors cannot raise the logic voltage.
Board pin: verify 5V/VBUS is USB-powered; a pin labelled VIN may be input-only.
