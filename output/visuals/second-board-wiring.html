Keep external +5 V separate from ESP32 USB/VIN/3V3. Only ground is joined in this plan. Do not power the servo from 3V3.

ECHO voltage divider

Use 3 × 10 kΩ + 1 × 1 kΩ. The junction gives about 3.23 V from a 5 V ECHO signal. Never connect ECHO directly to GPIO27.

220 Ω resistors are not needed. Each resistor must span separate breadboard nodes; its legs must not share the same connected strip.

Upload and configure

  1. Disconnect the mic board’s USB; connect only the second ESP32. Choose PlatformIO → Project Tasks → actuator_board → Upload.
  2. Open serial at 115200 baud, press EN, and open the printed Cabinet control page: http://<ip>/ on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Test with the horn removed first. Defaults: 90° rest → 0° press → 90° return, 0.5 s each way and 0.5 s hold. Adjust endpoints and timing on the page to fit the actual button.
  4. Reconnect the mic. Automatic trigger: ≥90 dB for 3 seconds; one press per episode. Rearm after return and 3 seconds below 88 dB. Brief peaks and stale readings do not trigger.

Commanded degrees need mechanical calibration for your SG90. Use a harmless mock button; there is no button-contact feedback.

Independent door monitoring

Open above 20 cm. Closes at or below 18 cm after filtering; no echo = unknown. Adjust the threshold on the page. Door state never triggers or blocks the servo.

Mount so opening the door changes the measured distance and still gives an echo. A fixed sensor facing a fixed wall will not detect door movement.
